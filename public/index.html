What is Forever Stories?

Forever Stories is a personal memory preservation app that helps you capture and share your life experiences with family and loved ones. Answer daily prompts, write free-form stories, and invite family members to read and cherish your memories.

Daily Prompts

Receive thoughtful questions each day designed to help you recall and record meaningful life moments.

Share with Family

Invite loved ones to read your stories and even submit questions they'd like you to answer.

Photos & Videos

Attach photos and videos to bring your stories to life and make them even more meaningful.

Your Legacy

Build a collection of stories that your family can treasure for generations to come.

FAQ

How do I get started?
Download the app from the App Store, create an account, and start answering your first prompt!

How do I invite family?
Go to Settings > Loved Ones Access and send an invite via email or text message.
