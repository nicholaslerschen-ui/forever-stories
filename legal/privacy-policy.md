# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: February 12, 2026**

Forever Stories ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
When you create an account, we collect:
- **Name**: Your full name for personalization
- **Email Address**: For account authentication and communications
- **Phone Number**: Optional, for SMS notifications
- **Password**: Encrypted and securely stored

### Content You Create
- **Stories and Memories**: Text content you write
- **Photos and Videos**: Media files you attach to stories
- **Voice Recordings**: Optional audio responses
- **AI Chat History**: Conversations with AI persona
- **Questions Submitted**: Questions you submit to story owners

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Usage Data**: Features used, time spent, interaction patterns
- **Log Data**: IP address, access times, error logs
- **Push Notification Tokens**: For sending notifications

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

### Provide Services
- Authenticate your account and maintain security
- Store and display your stories and memories
- Generate AI personas based on your content
- Facilitate sharing stories with invited family members
- Send daily writing prompts
- Process photo and video uploads

### Communications
- Send welcome emails when you create an account
- Send invitations to family members on your behalf
- Send push notifications for new questions and responses
- Send service updates and important announcements
- Respond to support requests

### Improve Services
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Identify and fix technical issues
- Develop new features based on user needs
- Enhance AI persona accuracy and quality

### Legal Compliance
- Comply with legal obligations
- Enforce our Terms of Service
- Protect against fraud and abuse
- Respond to legal requests

## 3. How We Share Your Information

### With Your Consent
- **Invited Family Members**: People you explicitly invite can view stories you've shared
- **AI Persona Interactions**: Viewers can interact with AI trained on your stories

### Service Providers
We share data with trusted third-party services:
- **AWS S3**: Cloud storage for photos and videos
- **Supabase**: Database hosting for account and story data
- **Anthropic**: AI model provider for persona generation
- **Twilio**: SMS delivery for text message invitations
- **AWS SES**: Email delivery for notifications and invitations

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose information:
- To comply with legal process (subpoena, court order)
- To protect our rights and property
- To prevent fraud or security threats
- In connection with legal proceedings

### Business Transfers
If Forever Stories is acquired or merged, your information may be transferred to the new owner with notice to you.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### Storage Locations
- **Database**: Supabase (PostgreSQL on AWS)
- **Files**: AWS S3 (US West region)
- **Backups**: Automated daily backups retained for 30 days

### Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest
- **Password Protection**: Passwords hashed using bcrypt
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access to user data
- **Regular Audits**: Security reviews and vulnerability testing
- **Monitoring**: Automated monitoring for suspicious activity

### Data Retention
- **Active Accounts**: Data stored indefinitely
- **Deleted Accounts**: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
- **Backups**: Backup retention up to 30 days

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

### Access and Control
- **View Your Data**: Access all your stories and account information
- **Download Your Data**: Export your stories and media files
- **Delete Your Data**: Request account and data deletion
- **Update Information**: Modify your profile and email address
- **Manage Permissions**: Control who can view your stories

### Communication Preferences
- **Email Notifications**: Opt out of marketing emails
- **Push Notifications**: Disable in app settings or device settings
- **SMS Messages**: Opt out by replying STOP to text invitations

### Account Deletion
To delete your account:
1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account
2. Confirm deletion request
3. All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days
4. Invited viewers will lose access to your stories

## 6. Children's Privacy

Forever Stories is available to users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we discover we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

Parents who believe their child under 13 has created an account should contact us at support@foreverstories.com.

## 7. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:
- **Right to Know**: What personal information we collect and how it's used
- **Right to Delete**: Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out**: Opt out of sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: Equal service regardless of privacy requests

To exercise these rights, contact support@foreverstories.com.

## 8. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

European Union residents have rights under GDPR:
- **Right of Access**: Obtain copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure**: Request deletion of data
- **Right to Restrict Processing**: Limit how we use data
- **Right to Data Portability**: Receive data in machine-readable format
- **Right to Object**: Object to certain data processing
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent at any time

Contact support@foreverstories.com to exercise these rights.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Forever Stories operates in the United States. If you access from outside the US:
- Your data will be transferred to and stored in the United States
- US privacy laws may differ from your country's laws
- By using our service, you consent to this transfer
- We implement safeguards to protect your data during transfer

## 12. AI and Machine Learning

### AI Persona Generation
- AI personas are created using Anthropic's Claude AI model
- Stories you write are processed to train your personal AI persona
- AI-generated content is not stored separately from your stories
- Viewers can interact with your AI persona if you grant access

### Data Processing
- Story content is sent to Anthropic API for AI processing
- Anthropic's privacy policy and data handling practices apply
- We do not use your stories to train general AI models
- Your AI persona is unique to your account
